# Viral Moment Editorial Scoring

This is a transparent editorial rubric, not a prediction of virality.

| Dimension | Weight | Evaluation |
|---|---:|---|
| Hook / opening pull | 15 | Does the opening create a clear reason to continue? |
| Payoff / satisfaction | 15 | Is there a complete, satisfying answer, reveal, joke, result, or reaction? |
| Clarity for a new viewer | 15 | Can someone unfamiliar with the source understand it? |
| Context independence | 10 | Does it stand alone without distorting the original meaning? |
| Curiosity / tension / progression | 10 | Does the moment develop rather than merely state? |
| Emotional / entertainment value | 10 | Is there a meaningful human, funny, dramatic, or entertaining element? |
| Pacing / conciseness | 10 | Is each included beat doing useful work? |
| Specificity / usefulness / novelty | 5 | Is there a concrete insight, detail, or distinctive moment? |
| Visual support | 5 | Do visuals clarify, demonstrate, or strengthen the moment? |
| Share / discussion potential | 5 | Is there a natural reason someone might share or discuss it? |

## Penalties and hard flags

Flag missing context, misleading hooks, incomplete payoff, repetition, filler, inaudible key speech, privacy concerns, unclear visual evidence, near-duplicates, and cuts that change meaning. A severe integrity issue can disqualify a candidate regardless of numeric score.

## Reporting

Show dimension scores, total, evidence, confidence, and risks. Do not represent the score as a probability or promise. Thresholds should be configurable and calibrated through human review; do not invent a universal threshold.
